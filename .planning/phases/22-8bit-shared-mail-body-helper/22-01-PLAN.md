| T-22-01 | Tampering | `load_smtp_config` config parsing | low | mitigate | Allow-list match on `smtp_encoding` values (only `"8bit"` and `"quoted-printable"` recognized); unknown values fall back to `QuotedPrintable` with a `tracing::warn!`. | T-22-02 | Info Disclosure | Debug/log output of `SmtpConfig` | medium | mitigate | Do NOT add `#[derive(Debug)]` to `SmtpConfig` in this plan (would leak `pass`). The `tracing::warn!` for unknown encoding logs only `value = %other` (the offending string), not the full struct. |
| T-22-03 | Denial of Service | Unknown config value causes mail to fail | low | accept | Rejected by design: tolerant fallback (D-08) means a typo cannot disable mail — it silently reverts to QP. Trade-off is deliberate and mirrors the existing `smtp_tls` policy. |
