Wire the Plan-02 primitives (`sanitize_html`, `RenderedContent.body_html`) and the Plan-03 MIME extension (`build_message(..., html_body, ...)`) through the entire mail send stack: service create_job + test-mail-with-body + template create/update, worker rendered_html_body persistence + MIME assembly, and REST DTOs for both mail jobs and mail templates.
